The 18 deferred cases comprise four C0-accepted and fourteen C0-rejected programs.
They are excluded from the matched safety denominator. All 93 parse successfully;
full C0 checking supplies the ownership oracle. Real tooling-index acceptance of
double release is deliberately rejected as enforcing evidence. C0 does not expose
its internal owner lattice: the expected states are independently source-authored,
paired with full C0 outcomes rather than copied from the native observation.

Native diagnostics match the authored source spans. Two C0 phase anchors differ:
`return-moved` anchors the return statement rather than its moved operand;
`class-null-call-loan` anchors the earlier borrow rather than the later move.
The remaining marked C0 anchors fall inside their authored native fault spans.

source span, function declaration and function ordinal while preserving the
original syntax dump. Lifetime tests cover equal-content distinct programs,
wrong origins/function/slot/loan IDs, retained source survival, and 32 repeated
row-limit, step-limit, invalid-bound, negative and ill-typed constructions.

## Implementation and independent boundaries

Ordinary finite Classes reuse nominal Class storage plans and module-owned
LLVM layout caches. Private visibility is normalized only for physical storage
shape. Ordinary instances have their actual payload size, field offsets and
alignment; they do not acquire the private Error header. Reverse declared
cleanup retires owned numeric buffers, finite child Classes and readonly text
snapshots before retiring their containing object. Error identity and its
approved private ABI remain separate.

Every field operation records its real source receiver SymbolId, Class TypeId,
storage slot, field symbol, ordinal, byte offset, cleanup rank and constructor
state. The independent consumer reconstructs those facts from the source and
layout. It rejects unknown receiver slots, readonly replacement and unsafe
roots. Constructors establish each declared field once before receiver escape;
the current verified subset permits parameter moves, literals, numeric buffer
allocation and already initialized field reads. Unsupported constructor shapes
remain deferred.

Field replacement evaluates the RHS before committing the new value and
retiring the prior owner. CFG success/error edges and SSA ownership preserve
the old field on a checked-call error. Explicit nullable receiver guards precede
RHS effects, matching C0. Buffer exchange captures both complete descriptors
before either store, including identical slots and aliasing receivers.
Borrowed field call arguments cannot accompany consumption or replacement of
their containing owner. No `noalias` premise or borrowed-local owner root is
invented.

Copied scalar, boolean and proven static-literal string call results may be
discarded as statements. Calls still execute, including false boolean results
and checked error propagation. Owned result discard remains subject to C0's
explicit policy. Scalar field and buffer-length getters are accepted only with
their original single-return source projection and a separate completed-binding
consumer; arbitrary method bodies are not replaced.

Typecore, RecoverFlow, Core, CFG, SSA and LLVM verification remain independent.
New semantic mutations cover receiver identity, nominal layout, field slots,
initialization, mutability, cleanup and stage ownership. Physical alternatives
must first pass LLVM's own verifier before the Astra physical verifier rejects
them. Independent C/C++ layout probes, C0 controls and test-only allocation
traces have no Astra-native source-program credit.
